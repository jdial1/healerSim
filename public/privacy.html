Privacy Policy for Aegis

Last updated: 5 September 2026

Aegis is a single-player game. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash-reporting SDK.

Data the app stores

Your progress — character level, experience, talent choices, completed dungeons, and action bar layout — is saved on your own device so the game can resume where you left off. Nothing else is recorded.

  • Android app: stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
  • Web version: stored in your browser's local storage. Clearing site data for this site deletes it.

Data sent off your device

None. The Android app requests no permissions at all and contains no networking code; it works fully in airplane mode. The web version is a static page and sends your progress nowhere.

One indirect exception is worth naming: if you have Android's system backup enabled, Android may copy your saved progress to your own Google account as part of the standard device backup, the same as it does for other apps. That transfer is performed by Android, not by Aegis, and is covered by Google's privacy policy. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

Children

Aegis is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, including children.
